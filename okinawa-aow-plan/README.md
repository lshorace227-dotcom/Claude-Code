# Okinawa PADI Advanced Open Water (AOW) — Full Plan

**Starting point:** PADI Open Water Diver, certified 18 August 2023 (Liuqiu Dive, Pingtung, Taiwan).
**Goal:** PADI Advanced Open Water Diver, earned in Okinawa.
**Document date:** August 2026. All prices are as advertised by the shops and **must be re-confirmed at booking** — Okinawa shops change rates seasonally.

---

## 0. Read this first — the one real risk in your plan

You certified in **August 2023**. Unless you have been diving regularly since, you are roughly **three years out of the water**. This matters more than anything else in this plan:

- **You are still eligible.** PADI AOW requires only a PADI Open Water certification (or a qualifying cert from another agency) and age 15+. There is **no minimum number of logged dives**. Nobody will stop you from signing up.
- **But AOW is not a re-learning course.** Day 1 dive 1 is a *deep dive to 30 m*. If your buoyancy, trim and air consumption are rusty, you will spend the course fighting your own body instead of learning navigation and gas management. That is the difference between a card and an actual skill upgrade.

**Recommendation:** budget **one extra half-day for a refresher** before the course starts.

| Option | What it is | Rough cost |
|---|---|---|
| **PADI ReActivate** (recommended) | eLearning knowledge refresher + in-water skills session with an instructor. Puts a "ReActivated Thru" date on your card. | eLearning ~USD 63 + shop in-water fee (varies) |
| Shop "refresher" / 復習潛 | Same idea, no new card. Most Okinawa shops offer this. | ~¥8,000–15,000 |
| 2 fun dives before the course | Cheapest, least formal, works fine if you were a confident OW diver | ~¥15,000–20,000 |

Tell the shop up front: *"My last dive was in 2023, I have roughly N logged dives."* A good shop will build a refresher in. A shop that shrugs at that answer is the wrong shop.

## 1. What the AOW course actually is

### Structure — 5 Adventure Dives, typically over 2 days

| # | Dive | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Deep Dive** (18–30 m) | **Mandatory** |
| 2 | **Underwater Navigation** (compass + natural nav) | **Mandatory** |
| 3–5 | Three electives from ~14 Adventure Dives | Your choice (subject to what the shop runs) |

### Knowledge development
- **PADI Adventures in Diving eLearning**, roughly 6–8 hours of self-study, list price around **USD 231** (PADI Club members get 20% off). Many Okinawa shops **include this in the course price** — check, because it is a big chunk of the total.
- Knowledge reviews + short quizzes per Adventure Dive. **There is no final exam.**

### What the card gets you
- Recreational depth limit goes from **18 m → 30 m**.
- Each Adventure Dive **counts as the first dive of the matching Specialty course** (e.g. your Deep Adventure Dive credits toward the Deep Diver specialty).
- Prerequisite for **Rescue Diver**, which is the course most experienced divers say actually changed how they dive.
- Access to boat trips and sites that gate on AOW (deeper Kerama sites, most wreck dives).

### Which 3 electives to choose in Okinawa

| Elective | Verdict for you |
|---|---|
| **Peak Performance Buoyancy (PPB)** | **Take this one.** Highest practical value, and it directly fixes 3-years-rusty buoyancy. Non-negotiable in my view. |
| **Night Dive** | **Strongly recommended.** Okinawa night diving (Cape Maeda, Sunabe) is excellent and it is the dive you are least likely to organise on your own later. |
| **Boat Dive** | Useful if you are doing Kerama/Minna boat trips — easy, and it counts. |
| **Underwater Naturalist / Fish ID** | Good fit for Okinawa reef life. Low effort. |
| **Digital Underwater Photography** | Fun, but only if you already own/rent a camera. Low skill payoff. |
| **Drift Dive** | Good if the shop runs Kerama channel sites. |
| **Search & Recovery / DSMB** | Genuinely useful skills; DSMB especially for boat diving in Asia. |
| **Wreck Dive** | Ask before assuming. The famous Okinawa wreck (**USS Emmons**) sits at **~40 m** and is a technical/deep-specialty dive — **it is not available to an AOW student.** Shops that offer a "wreck" Adventure Dive use shallower wrecks. |

**My suggested set: Peak Performance Buoyancy + Night + Boat (or Drift, if Kerama).**

## 2. Where in Okinawa to base yourself

| Area | Dive sites | Why choose it | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Onna Village (恩納村)** | Cape Maeda / **Blue Cave**, Horseshoe Cliff, Mermaid Grotto | The densest cluster of dive shops on the island; resort hotels; shore-dive access so courses run cheap and flexible | Blue Cave is **extremely crowded in summer**, and shore entries get cancelled on wind/swell |
| **Naha / Tomari Port** | **Kerama Islands** (day-boat, ~50 min high-speed ferry / dive boat) | **The best water in mainland-accessible Okinawa** — visibility often **20–40 m**, turtles, healthy coral. Best city base, airport 25 min by Yui Rail | Full-day boat commitment; seasickness; more expensive |
| **Chatan / Sunabe (北谷・砂辺)** | Sunabe seawall | Great macro and **night diving**, American Village nightlife/food, several long-running English shops | Less dramatic topography |
| **Motobu / Sesoko / Minna / Ie Island** | Sesoko, Minna, Ie | Quieter, boat diving, near Churaumi Aquarium | Furthest from Naha airport (~2 h) |

**Recommendation:** base in **Onna** if you want the simplest, cheapest, most flexible course, or **Naha** if you want your five dives to be Kerama boat dives. If budget allows, do the course in Onna and add **one Kerama fun-dive day** after certification — that way your first 30 m dives happen in easy water and your best water gets dived with a card in hand.

## 5. When to go

| Period | Water temp | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **Apr–Jun** | 23–27 °C | **Prime.** Stable weather, post-rainy-season clarity building |
| **Jul–Aug** | 27–29 °C | Warmest water and best coral colour, but **peak typhoon season** — highest cancellation risk, and Blue Cave is packed |
| **Sep–early Oct** | 27–28 °C | Still typhoon-exposed, but crowds thin fast |
| **Oct–Nov** | **24–27 °C** | **My pick.** Warm water, **excellent visibility**, typhoons mostly gone, far fewer divers. Widely considered the real best season by locals |
| **Dec–Mar** | 20–23 °C | 5 mm wetsuit or semi-dry; humpback whale season in Kerama; fewer course days run |

**Kerama visibility runs 20–40 m in good conditions.**

**It is currently early August 2026.** If you can be flexible, **target late October or November 2026**: better visibility, lower prices, near-zero typhoon risk, and a 3 mm suit is still comfortable. If you must go in August/September, **build in buffer days and book refundable accommodation** — Blue Cave tours cancel on wind and swell, and shops will reschedule you rather than dive an unsafe site.

## 6. Trip length and sample itinerary

**Absolute minimum: 4 days / 3 nights. Recommended: 5 days / 4 nights.**

The binding constraint is the **no-fly rule after diving: 18 hours minimum, and Okinawa shops generally enforce 24 hours.** Because AOW includes a 30 m deep dive, **plan a full 24 hours dry before your flight.** You can dive on your arrival day; you cannot dive on your departure day.

### Recommended 5D4N (Onna base)

| Day | Plan |
|---|---|
| **Day 1** | Fly into Naha. Transfer to Onna (~1 h by shop pickup, bus, or taxi). Meet the shop, gear fitting, finish any remaining eLearning. Optional: **afternoon refresher dive** |
| **Day 2** | **AOW Day 1** — Dive 1 **Deep (Cape Maeda wall / Blue Cave)**, Dive 2 **Navigation**, Dive 3 **Peak Performance Buoyancy**. Evening: log dives, briefing for night dive |
| **Day 3** | **AOW Day 2** — Dive 4 **Boat / Drift / Naturalist**, Dive 5 **Night Dive** (or run the night dive on Day 2 evening and finish earlier on Day 3). **Certification issued — temporary card same day, physical/digital card follows** |
| **Day 4** | **Either** a **Kerama fun-dive day** (2–3 dives, ¥18,000–23,000) as your first dives *as* an AOW diver — **or**, if weather cancelled anything, this is your **make-up day**. If you dive today, your flight must be Day 5 afternoon or later |
| **Day 5** | **No diving.** Churaumi Aquarium, Shuri Castle, Kokusai-dori, American Village. Fly home |

**Do not book a 3-day trip.** One typhoon warning or one blown-out sea state and you leave Okinawa uncertified.

## 7. Pre-trip checklist

### 8 weeks out
- [ ] Pick your shop from §3 — **email 2–3 for quotes** (template in §8)
- [ ] Confirm **exactly what is included**: eLearning, gear, cert fee, lunch, transfers, boat fees, tax
- [ ] Confirm **whether they can run your chosen electives** on your dates (Night dive especially — not every shop runs it every day)
- [ ] Book flights and accommodation — **refundable**, if travelling Jul–Sep

### 4 weeks out
- [ ] Buy and complete the **Adventures in Diving eLearning** (6–8 h) — do this *before* you fly, not in your hotel room at 1 a.m.
- [ ] Complete the **RSTC / PADI Medical Statement**. Every Japanese shop requires this; refusing means you don't dive. **If you answer "yes" to any question, you need a doctor's clearance letter — in English or Japanese — carried with you.** Arrange this early; it cannot be fixed on arrival
- [ ] Buy **dive insurance** — DAN Asia-Pacific or DiveAssure. Shops strongly recommend it and some require proof. Confirm your travel insurance doesn't exclude scuba beyond 18 m

### 2 weeks out
- [ ] **Do a refresher** at home if you can — a local pool session is worth more than the first day of the course
- [ ] Service or test any personal gear (computer battery, mask fit)

### Packing
- [ ] **OW certification card** — the digital card in the **PADI app** is accepted; bring a screenshot as backup in case of no signal
- [ ] **Logbook** (or your digital log) — shops will ask for total dive count and last dive date
- [ ] Passport, insurance documents, medical clearance letter if applicable
- [ ] Own mask (the single highest-value personal item), swimwear, rash guard, towel
- [ ] Reef-safe sunscreen, motion sickness tablets (**essential for Kerama boat days**), seasickness-friendly breakfast plan
- [ ] Cash — many Okinawan shops are **cash-preferred**; confirm card acceptance before you arrive

### Getting around
Most shops offer **hotel pickup** (Piranha charges ¥500 each way within Onna; Breathe Time includes transfers during the course). **Use it.** If you are considering renting a car instead, **verify in advance that your driving licence is valid in Japan** — Japan only recognises specific International Driving Permits and a short list of foreign licences with official translations. Do not assume; check before you book a car.

## 9. After you certify

1. **Log the dives properly** — depth, time, gas, conditions, weights. This is the record every future shop will ask for.
2. **Dive again within 6 months.** The reason your AOW is a stretch right now is a 3-year gap; don't repeat it.
3. **Next steps, in order of actual value:** *Rescue Diver + EFR* (the course that most changes how you dive) → *Enriched Air Nitrox* (cheap, no dives required, extends bottom time on repetitive days) → *Deep Diver specialty* (to 40 m).
4. **Where to use the card:** Kerama Islands, then **Miyako-jima** (caves and arches — world-class) and **Yonaguni** (the underwater monument, winter hammerhead season). Both are a short domestic hop from Naha and both open up considerably with AOW.
