# 05 · Daily Life Schedule

A schedule you'll actually keep beats a perfect one you'll abandon in week
three. This is built around a demanding desk job with irregular evenings —
adjust the clock times to your reality, but keep the *structure* and the
*order of operations*.

---

under 6 hours while dieting shifts your weight loss substantially away from
fat and toward muscle — the exact opposite of this plan's entire purpose.

**Sunday meal prep is the load-bearing habit of this entire plan.** Nearly
every diet failure is a decision made while hungry with no good option
available. Two hours on Sunday removes that decision from your week
entirely.

## Daily non-negotiables

Whatever else falls apart in a given day, hit these five:

- [ ] **170 g protein**
- [ ] **10 min posture routine**
- [ ] **8,000+ steps**
- [ ] **3 L water**
- [ ] **7.5 h sleep**

Miss a workout — fine, it happens, get the next one. Miss these five
repeatedly and the plan stops working regardless of what else you do.

## When the day goes wrong

It will. Here's the triage, in priority order.

**Worked until 21:00, no gym.**
Do the 25-minute home session ([`03`](03-training-program.md#home--travel-fallback)).
If even that's impossible, do the posture routine and hit your protein. One
missed session in 30 weeks is 0.8% of the program. It is genuinely nothing.
Two missed sessions in a row is a pattern — that's the one to watch.

**Business dinner tonight.**
Run rest-day macros for breakfast and lunch to bank calories. Eat 30 g
protein before you go. Protein and vegetables first at the table. See
[`02-nutrition.md`](02-nutrition.md#business-dinners-and-drinking).

**Travelling.**
Resistance band in the bag. Hotel room workout. Walk the city instead of
taking taxis. Aim to *maintain* rather than progress — that's a win on the
road.

**Sick.**
Stop training. Eat at maintenance, keep protein high, sleep. Training
through illness in a calorie deficit extends the illness and costs you more
days than resting does.

**Bad week — ate poorly, skipped everything, feel like you've undone it.**
You haven't. Fat loss is measured over months. A bad week costs you about
four days of progress. The genuinely damaging move is deciding the plan is
broken and abandoning it. **Restart Monday. Don't compensate, don't
punish, don't "make up for it" with an extreme week.** Just resume.

## Habit stacking order

Don't launch all of this on day one. Build it in layers — each one anchored
to something you already do reliably.

| Weeks | Add | Anchor it to |
|---|---|---|
| **1–2** | Posture routine + protein target | Posture right after waking; protein at every existing meal |
| **3–4** | Training 4×/week + step target | Fixed gym days in the calendar as real appointments |
| **5–6** | Full calorie tracking + Sunday meal prep | Log immediately after eating, prep at a fixed Sunday hour |
| **7–8** | Sleep schedule + hourly desk resets | 22:00 phone alarm; recurring hourly work-calendar alert |

By week 8 the whole system runs on habit rather than willpower. Which
matters, because willpower is a finite resource and 30 weeks is a long
time.
