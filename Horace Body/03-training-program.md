# 03 · Training Program

30 weeks, three phases. 4 lifting days, 1 conditioning day, 1 active day,
1 full rest day.

Every session is built around two jobs at once: **protect the 57.8 kg of
muscle you already have**, and **pull your posture back into line**. The
pulling volume deliberately exceeds the pushing volume — that's the
correction, baked into the program rather than bolted on.

---

## Weekly split

| Day | Session | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| **Mon** | Upper A — Pull focus | 60 min |
| **Tue** | Lower A — Squat focus | 60 min |
| **Wed** | Zone 2 cardio + mobility | 45 min |
| **Thu** | Upper B — Push focus | 60 min |
| **Fri** | Lower B — Hinge focus + conditioning | 65 min |
| **Sat** | Football / hike / long walk | 60–90 min |
| **Sun** | Rest + posture routine only | 15 min |

---

## Universal rules

**Warm-up (8 min, every session)**
1. 5 min bike or brisk walk
2. Cat-cow × 10
3. World's greatest stretch × 5/side
4. Band pull-apart × 20
5. Two ramp-up sets of the first exercise at 50% and 75% load

**Load selection.** Pick a weight where the final rep of the set is hard but
clean. Leave 1–2 reps in reserve on every working set. Form breakdown means
the weight is too heavy — always, no exceptions.

**Progression.** When you hit the top of the rep range on every set of an
exercise, add 2.5 kg (upper body) or 5 kg (lower body) next session. In a
calorie deficit, progress will be slow and will eventually stall. **Holding
your loads steady while losing fat is a win, not a plateau.**

**Rest.** 2–3 min on compounds, 60–90 s on isolation work.

**Tempo.** 2 seconds down, no pause, controlled up. Never bounce.

# PHASE 1 — Foundation & Posture Reset
### Weeks 1–8

Groove the movements, build the corrective habit, start the deficit. Loads
are moderate. **Do not chase weight in this phase** — chase clean reps and
positions.

## Monday — Upper A (Pull)

| # | Exercise | Sets × Reps | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Dead hang from bar | 3 × 30 s | Decompresses the spine. Start here every Monday. |
| 2 | Chest-supported row | 4 × 10–12 | Chest on pad removes lower-back cheat. Squeeze shoulder blades 1 s. |
| 3 | Lat pulldown (or assisted pull-up) | 4 × 8–10 | Pull to upper chest, elbows down and back |
| 4 | Single-arm dumbbell row | 3 × 12/side | Full stretch at the bottom |
| 5 | **Face pull** | 4 × 15–20 | *The single most important exercise in this program for your posture.* Pull to forehead, thumbs back, hold 1 s. |
| 6 | Prone Y-T-W raise | 3 × 8 each letter | Light or no weight. Lower/mid trap activation. |
| 7 | Chin tuck | 3 × 10 × 5 s hold | Deep neck flexors. Directly counters forward head. |
| 8 | Biceps curl | 3 × 12 | |

## Tuesday — Lower A (Squat)

| # | Exercise | Sets × Reps | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Goblet squat | 4 × 10 | Elbows inside knees, upright torso |
| 2 | Bulgarian split squat | 3 × 10/leg | Brutal, and the best single fix for hip and leg asymmetry |
| 3 | Leg press | 3 × 12 | Don't let the lower back round at the bottom |
| 4 | Lying leg curl | 3 × 12 | Hamstrings — underactive in your pattern |
| 5 | Standing calf raise | 3 × 15 | Full stretch at the bottom |
| 6 | **Dead bug** | 3 × 8/side | Deep core, anti-extension. Low back stays flat on the floor. |
| 7 | Side plank | 3 × 30 s/side | Glute medius + obliques |

## Wednesday — Zone 2 + Mobility

- **30 min Zone 2 cardio.** Bike, incline walk, rowing, or swimming.
  Intensity: you can hold a conversation but wouldn't want to sing. Heart
  rate roughly 180 − age = **~132 bpm**. This burns fat without adding
  recovery debt.
- **15 min mobility:** the full routine from
  [`04-posture-protocol.md`](04-posture-protocol.md), taken slowly.

## Thursday — Upper B (Push)

| # | Exercise | Sets × Reps | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Incline dumbbell press | 4 × 10 | Incline over flat — builds upper chest, less shoulder-forward pull |
| 2 | Standing dumbbell shoulder press | 4 × 10 | Standing forces core and posture engagement |
| 3 | Push-up | 3 × AMRAP | Push all the way through at the top — that's serratus |
| 4 | **Cable lateral raise** | 4 × 15 | Side delts. This is what builds the shoulder-to-waist V. |
| 5 | Rear delt fly | 3 × 15 | More rear delt than front. Deliberate. |
| 6 | Serratus wall slide | 3 × 10 | Forearms on wall, slide up, reach at the top |
| 7 | Triceps rope pushdown | 3 × 12 | |

## Friday — Lower B (Hinge) + Conditioning

| # | Exercise | Sets × Reps | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Romanian deadlift | 4 × 8–10 | Hinge at hips, soft knees, flat back. Hamstrings and glutes. |
| 2 | **Hip thrust** | 4 × 10–12 | Best glute builder there is. Glutes are the direct antidote to anterior pelvic tilt. |
| 3 | Reverse lunge | 3 × 10/leg | Easier on the knees than forward lunges |
| 4 | Leg curl (or Nordic negative) | 3 × 10 | |
| 5 | **Pallof press** | 3 × 12/side | Anti-rotation core. Teaches a neutral ribcage over pelvis. |
| 6 | Hanging knee raise | 3 × 12 | Lower abs. Curl the pelvis up — don't just swing the legs. |

**Conditioning finisher — 15 min**
10 rounds: 30 s hard / 60 s easy on bike or rower. Hard means you couldn't
speak more than three words.

## Saturday — Play

Football if you can get a game — it's the most on-theme option available
and it's cardio you'll actually look forward to. Otherwise: 60–90 min hike,
long walk, swim, or badminton.

## Sunday — Rest

Full rest from lifting. Posture routine only (10 min). Walk if you want to.
Meal prep for the week.

# PHASE 2 — Build & Cut
### Weeks 9–20

Same split, heavier loads, lower reps on the compounds. This is where the
visible change accumulates. **Week 9 is a diet break week** — eat at
maintenance, train normally.

### What changes from Phase 1

**Rep scheme on main lifts:** 4 × 6–8 instead of 4 × 10–12. Heavier weight.
Heavier lifting in a deficit is one of the strongest muscle-retention
signals you can send.

**Exercise upgrades**

| Phase 1 | → | Phase 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Goblet squat | → | Barbell back squat 4 × 6–8 |
| Lat pulldown | → | Weighted pull-up (or continue assisted) 4 × 6–8 |
| Incline dumbbell press | → | Barbell bench press 4 × 6–8 |
| Romanian deadlift | → | Conventional deadlift 4 × 5 (Fridays, alternate weeks with RDL) |
| Dumbbell shoulder press | → | Barbell overhead press 4 × 6–8 |

**Volume added:** one extra set on face pulls (5 × 15–20) and lateral
raises (5 × 15). These two drive the visual shape you're after — wide
shoulders, open chest — more than anything else in the program.

**Conditioning:** Friday finisher goes to 12 rounds. Wednesday Zone 2 goes
to 40 min.

**Keep unchanged:** all corrective work — face pulls, Y-T-W, chin tucks,
wall slides, dead bugs, Pallof. These never get dropped. Ever.

---

# PHASE 3 — Reveal
### Weeks 21–30

Final lean-out. The muscle is built; now you strip the last layer.
**Week 27 is a diet break week.**

### What changes

**Lifting: maintain, don't chase.** Drop to 3 × 6–8 on main lifts. You're
running on lower calories and your recovery is reduced. The goal now is
*keeping* strength, not adding it. If your lifts hold steady through Phase
3, you did it right.

**Cardio increases**
- Wednesday: 45 min Zone 2
- Saturday: add 30 min Zone 2 on top of your activity
- Steps: raise to 12,000 daily

**Add a fifth lift day (optional, Saturday)** — a short 30 min arms and
shoulders session, if recovery allows. Cut it immediately if sleep or
performance slips.

**Final 4 weeks:** hold the line. Nothing new, nothing fancy. This is where
most people quit or start doing something stupid. Just execute.

## Home / travel fallback

Business trip, no gym, hotel room. No excuses needed — 25 minutes:

| Exercise | Sets × Reps |
|---|---|
| Push-up | 4 × 15–20 |
| Bodyweight squat | 4 × 20 |
| Reverse lunge | 3 × 12/leg |
| Pike push-up | 3 × 10 |
| Glute bridge | 3 × 20 |
| Superman hold | 3 × 30 s |
| Plank | 3 × 45 s |
| Dead bug | 3 × 10/side |

Pack a **resistance band**. It weighs nothing, fits in a laptop bag, and it
lets you do band pull-aparts and face pulls anywhere — which are the two
things you least want to miss.

---

## If you only have 3 days a week

Life happens. Compress to full-body:

**Day 1:** Squat · Row · Bench · Face pull · Dead bug
**Day 2:** RDL · Pull-up · Overhead press · Lateral raise · Pallof press
**Day 3:** Hip thrust · Chest-supported row · Incline press · Face pull · Hanging knee raise

3 × 8–10 on everything, 4 × 15 on face pulls. This is not a failure mode —
it's a legitimate program that will still get you there, slightly slower.
