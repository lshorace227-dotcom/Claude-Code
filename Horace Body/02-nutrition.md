### Carb cycling — the only complexity worth having

Protein and fat stay flat every day. Carbs move. Put the food where the
work is.

| | Calories | Protein | Fat | Carbs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Training day** (×4) | 2,150 | 170 g | 65 g | 220 g |
| **Rest day** (×3) | 1,800 | 170 g | 65 g | 135 g |
| **Weekly average** | 2,050 | 170 g | 65 g | 184 g |

Weekly total: 14,350 kcal against a 17,850 kcal maintenance = **3,500 kcal
weekly deficit ≈ 0.45 kg fat/week.**

### Why these numbers

**Protein 170 g (2.1 g/kg target bodyweight).** This is the entire reason
you'll arrive at 72 kg lean instead of 72 kg soft. In a calorie deficit,
protein intake is the primary determinant of whether the weight you lose is
fat or muscle. 170 g is at the high end of the useful range, deliberately.
Split it across 4 meals at ~40 g each — muscle protein synthesis responds
better to distribution than to one large hit.

**Fat 65 g (0.8 g/kg).** The floor for hormone production, and specifically
for testosterone, which you want protected while dieting. Don't go lower to
"save calories" — it backfires.

**Carbs are the adjustable variable.** They fuel training, so they're
highest on training days. On rest days you simply need fewer. This isn't a
low-carb diet — 220 g on training days is plenty of rice and noodles.

## Meal template

### Training day — 2,150 kcal · 170 P / 65 F / 220 C

**Breakfast (07:00) — ~450 kcal, 40 P**
- 3 whole eggs + 2 egg whites, scrambled (~280 kcal, 26 P)
- 1 slice wholegrain bread or 1 small 馒头 (~120 kcal, 4 P)
- 200 ml unsweetened soy milk (~60 kcal, 6 P)
- Black coffee or tea

**Lunch (12:15) — ~650 kcal, 50 P**
- 150 g chicken breast / lean beef / fish (~200 kcal, 40 P)
- 150 g cooked rice (~200 kcal) — about one 碗
- Large portion of vegetables, lightly oiled (~150 kcal)
- Clear soup

**Pre-training (17:30) — ~250 kcal, 25 P**
- 1 scoop whey protein (~120 kcal, 24 P)
- 1 banana (~100 kcal)

**Dinner (20:30) — ~700 kcal, 50 P**
- 180 g protein — fish, chicken, lean pork, tofu (~250 kcal, 45 P)
- 150 g rice / noodles / sweet potato (~200 kcal)
- Big serving of greens (~150 kcal)
- Small amount of healthy fat — nuts, avocado, or cooking oil (~100 kcal)

**Optional evening (22:00) — ~100 kcal, 15 P**
- 150 g plain Greek yoghurt or casein shake

### Rest day — 1,800 kcal · 170 P / 65 F / 135 C

Same structure, minus the pre-training shake and banana, and halve the rice
at lunch and dinner. Protein stays identical.

## Business dinners and drinking

You work in finance. 应酬 is not optional and pretending otherwise makes a
plan that fails in week three. Handle it explicitly instead.

**Before you go**
- Eat 30–40 g of protein beforehand — a shake or yoghurt. You'll arrive
  without the hunger that drives the damage.
- On a dinner day, run the *rest day* macros for breakfast and lunch to bank
  calories.

**At the table**
- Protein and vegetables first. Fill up on those before rice and noodles
  arrive.
- Skip or dilute the dipping sauces. This is the highest-leverage single
  move at a Chinese table.
- Alcohol: 1 beer ≈ 150 kcal, 1 白酒 shot ≈ 80 kcal. If you must drink,
  count it and stop at 3. Alcohol also blunts muscle protein synthesis for
  ~24 h and wrecks sleep quality — the training cost exceeds the calorie
  cost.
- Water between every drink.

**After**
- No compensating by skipping the next day's meals. Return to normal macros
  immediately. One dinner does not undo a week; a week of guilt-driven
  chaos does.

### Your actual load: 2 dinners per week — and it's already priced in

Confirmed at **two 应酬 dinners per week.** Here's the arithmetic, so you can
stop wondering whether they're wrecking things.

A typical business dinner runs **1,100–1,400 kcal** against the 700 kcal
dinner the plan allocates. Bank calories during the day (below) and you claw
back ~350, leaving a net overage of roughly **350 kcal per dinner**.

| | kcal |
|---|---|
| Weekly deficit on 7 clean days | −3,500 |
| 2 business dinners, net overage | +700 |
| **Realized weekly deficit** | **−2,800** |

−2,800 kcal/week ≈ **0.36 kg of fat per week.** Over 30 weeks that's
**10.8 kg** — which is precisely the 82.8 → 72.0 kg target.

**So the 30-week timeline already assumes you eat two business dinners every
week.** They are budgeted, not tolerated. Nothing needs to change.

### The dinner-day protocol

On a day you know a 应酬 is coming:

| Meal | Normal training day | Dinner day |
|---|---|---|
| Breakfast | 450 kcal | 400 kcal — cut the bread/馒头 |
| Lunch | 650 kcal | 450 kcal — protein + vegetables, skip the rice |
| Pre-dinner | — | **30 g protein shake before you leave** |
| Dinner | 700 kcal | ~1,100 kcal budget |

The pre-dinner shake is the highest-leverage item on that list. Arriving
hungry is what turns a 1,100 kcal dinner into a 1,800 kcal one.

**Keep protein at 170 g regardless.** Business dinners are usually
protein-rich — fish, meat, tofu — so this is normally easy. The calories
come from oil, sauces, alcohol, and the late carbs, not the protein.

### If it ever becomes 3+ per week

Sustained, that adds ~350 kcal/week and pushes the timeline out by roughly
4–5 weeks. Not a failure — just arithmetic. Tell me if your schedule shifts

## Hydration, supplements, and the rest

**Water:** 3–3.5 L daily. Your body water reading (45.1 kg) is healthy —
keep it there. Dehydration also corrupts your next bioimpedance scan.

**Supplements worth taking**
| | Dose | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Creatine monohydrate | 5 g daily | Best-evidenced legal supplement there is. Helps retain strength in a deficit. Take it any time; timing is irrelevant. |
| Vitamin D3 | 2,000 IU daily | Office worker, limited sun. Common deficiency. |
| Whey protein | As needed | A tool for hitting 170 g, nothing more. |
| Fish oil | 1–2 g EPA+DHA | If you don't eat fatty fish twice a week. |

**Supplements not worth taking:** fat burners, BCAAs (redundant at 170 g
protein), testosterone boosters, detox teas, 减肥茶. All of it.

**Caffeine:** fine, useful pre-training. Cut-off at 14:00 — your sleep is a
training variable and caffeine has a ~6 hour half-life.

## Diet breaks

Every 8 weeks, take **1 week at maintenance** (~2,550 kcal, protein
unchanged). This isn't a cheat week — it's a planned reset that restores
leptin, thyroid output, and training performance, and makes the following
8-week block work better than grinding straight through.

Scheduled breaks: **Week 9 · Week 18 · Week 27**.

## Tracking

**Weeks 1–4: weigh and log everything.** Use MyFitnessPal or 薄荷健康. This
is tedious and it is temporary. You are calibrating your eye, not adopting
a permanent habit.

**Weeks 5+: log protein and total calories only.** By then you'll estimate
accurately enough.

**The check that actually matters:** if your weight hasn't moved in 2 weeks
*and* your waist hasn't moved, cut 150 kcal from carbs. Don't adjust based
on a single week — normal water fluctuation is larger than a week's fat
loss.
