# 06 · Progress Tracker

What to measure, how often, and how to read it without driving yourself
crazy.

---

## Measurement schedule

| Metric | Frequency | How |
|---|---|---|
| **Weight** | Daily | Morning, after toilet, before food/water, naked. **Record the 7-day average, not the daily number.** |
| **Waist** | Weekly (Sat) | At the navel, morning, unfed. Tape level and snug, not tight. Exhale normally. |
| **Photos** | Monthly (1st) | Front / back / both sides. Same spot, same light, same time of day, relaxed stance. |
| **Body scan** | Every 8 weeks | Same ioi 353 machine, same time of day, similar hydration. |
| **Strength** | Every session | Log weight × reps for the main lifts. |
| **Resting HR** | Weekly | On waking, before sitting up. |
| **Sleep** | Daily | Hours + subjective quality 1–5. |

### Why the 7-day weight average

Daily weight swings 1–2 kg from water, sodium, carbs, and digestion alone.
That's larger than a whole week of actual fat loss. If you react to daily
numbers you'll be making decisions from noise.

Compare **this week's average to last week's average.** That's the signal.

| **2027-03-06** | **30** | **72.0 kg** | **< 82 cm** | **13%** | **TARGET** |

**Muscle mass target at every checkpoint: ≥ 57.8 kg.** This is the number
that decides whether you arrive lean or merely smaller. If a scan shows
muscle dropping more than ~1 kg, you are losing weight too fast or eating
too little protein — raise calories by 200 and check your protein logging.

## Reading the data — decision rules

Use these instead of guessing. They cover almost everything that will come up.

**Weight fell more than 0.8 kg/week for two consecutive weeks**
→ Too fast. You're risking muscle. **Add 200 kcal of carbs.**

**Weight unchanged for 2 weeks AND waist unchanged**
→ Genuine stall. **Cut 150 kcal from carbs**, or add 2,000 daily steps.
Check your logging accuracy first — under-reporting is the usual culprit,
and cooking oil is the usual hiding place.

**Weight unchanged for 2 weeks BUT waist decreased**
→ **This is working.** You're losing fat and gaining or holding muscle.
Change nothing. This is a good outcome that looks like a bad one, and it's
the most common reason people quit a plan that's succeeding.

**Weight up 1–2 kg overnight**
→ Water. Salt, carbs, poor sleep, hard training, or stress. Ignore it
completely. It resolves in 2–4 days.

**Scan shows muscle mass dropped > 1 kg**
→ Raise calories 200 kcal. Verify protein is actually hitting 170 g. Check
you're sleeping 7.5 h. Ensure you're still lifting heavy, not drifting into
high-rep circuits.

**Strength dropping across multiple lifts for 2+ weeks**
→ Under-recovered. Take a **deload week**: same exercises, half the sets,
eat at maintenance. You'll come back stronger and the week costs you almost
nothing.

**Waist not moving but weight is falling**
→ Unusual. Check your tape measure technique first — inconsistent placement
is the most likely explanation.

## Checkpoint reviews — every 8 weeks

Do a full review at **Weeks 8, 16, 24, and 30**.

1. Full body scan on the same machine
2. Full photo set, all four angles
3. All measurements
4. Compare against the milestone table
5. **Take a diet break** (Weeks 9, 18, 27 — 1 week at maintenance)
6. Adjust calories for your new lower bodyweight

**Important:** as you get lighter, your TDEE falls. Roughly **−20 kcal of
maintenance per kg of bodyweight lost.** At 76 kg your TDEE will be near
2,400, not 2,550. Recalculate at each checkpoint or your deficit quietly
shrinks to nothing and you'll wonder why you stalled.

## Non-scale wins to log

The scale is a lagging, noisy, and emotionally unhelpful indicator. Track
these too — they often move first, and they're the things you actually
wanted.

- [ ] Belt moved in a notch
- [ ] Old shirt fits properly at the shoulders
- [ ] Someone comments that you look taller *(this is the posture work — it
      happens surprisingly early)*
- [ ] Jawline visible in photos
- [ ] Stairs without breathing hard
- [ ] Neck and shoulders no longer ache at end of day
- [ ] First unassisted pull-up, then first set of 10
- [ ] Upper abs visible in morning light
- [ ] Shirt tight on shoulders, loose at the waist
- [ ] Full abs visible
- [ ] Ear over shoulder in a relaxed side photo

## A note on the long middle

Weeks 10 through 22 are where this gets won or lost. The novelty is gone,
the finish line isn't in sight, and progress per week is small enough to be
invisible day to day.

Nothing is wrong when this happens. It's the normal shape of a 30-week
project. The only thing required in that stretch is that you keep executing
a plan that is already working.

Your adherence percentages in the weekly log matter more than any body
metric in that period. Hit 85%+ on the adherence lines and the body numbers
follow automatically — they have no choice.
