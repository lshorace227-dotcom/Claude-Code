weeks, before meaningful fat loss even shows up.** It's the fastest visual
win available to you, so it gets daily priority.

## The five rules

1. **Protein 170 g every single day.** Non-negotiable. This is what protects
   your 57.8 kg of muscle while you're in a deficit. Miss calories, never
   miss protein.
2. **Lift 4× per week.** The deficit tells your body to shed tissue; lifting
   tells it to shed fat and keep muscle. Without lifting you'll arrive at
   72 kg skinny, not lean.
3. **10 minutes of posture work daily.** Every day, including rest days.
   Compounding interest.
4. **8,000–10,000 steps daily.** Separate from training. This is most of
   your actual daily burn.
5. **7.5 hours of sleep.** Under 6 hours reliably wrecks fat loss and
   recovery. This is a training variable, not a lifestyle one.

## Honest caveats

- The posture read is from four photos, not a clinical exam. It's a strong
  visual pattern, not a diagnosis. **If you have pain — neck, shoulder, or
  low back — see a physiotherapist before starting the corrective work.**
- Bioimpedance scales (the ioi 353 included) carry roughly ±3–5% error on
  body fat percentage. Trust the *trend* across repeat scans on the same
  machine, not any single absolute number.
- The 550 kcal/day deficit the machine printed is reasonable, and this plan
  uses roughly that. It is not aggressive and it is not meant to be.
- I'm not a doctor. Get a basic blood panel before you start — fasting
  glucose, lipids, liver enzymes, thyroid. At 84 cm² visceral fat area it's
  worth having the baseline.
