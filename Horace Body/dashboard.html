The finding

Your April scan says something most people don't get to hear. 57.8 kg of muscle at 181 cm is above the standard range — most 28-year-old desk workers scan below it. Your protein (12.7 kg) sits at the top of normal.

So the plan is not to build a physique. It's to strip 10.8 kg of fat while losing none of the muscle you already own, and stack your skeleton back up straight.

Run the arithmetic: hold lean mass at 62.6 kg, drop to 72 kg bodyweight, and you land at 13.0% body fat. Bellingham is 186 cm / 75 kg — scaled to your 181 cm, that's ~71 kg. Your target and his build are the same number.
